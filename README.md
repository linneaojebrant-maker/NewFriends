# NewFriends

Final project for the Building AI course

## Summary

NeighborMatch is an AI-powered social platform designed to match newcomers based on shared interests, age, and family status (such as having children or a partner etc). It helps people build meaningful local friendships and organize group activities smoothly.

## Background

Moving to a new city or neighborhood is often an isolating experience. Building a new social network as an adult can be challenging, especially for newcomers.

* **Social Isolation:** Many adults find it difficult to make new friends after moving to a new area.
* **Parents Looking for Connections:** Families with young children often want to find local playdates and connect with other parents.
* **Finding Activity Partners:** It is hard to find nearby people who share specific hobbies, such as climbing, book clubs, or board games.

My personal motivation is to help people feel welcome in their new communities and create a simple way to form lasting friendships through shared real-life activities.

## How is it used?

Users create a profile specifying their basic demographics, location, interests, and whether they have children. 

1. **Profile Creation:** The user inputs their age, location, family status, and hobbies.
2. **AI Recommendation:** The matching algorithm finds compatible individuals or small groups nearby.
3. **Activity Planning:** The system suggests local activities tailored to the shared interests (e.g., "Park meetup for toddlers" or "Weekend hiking") by using open published events or other open sourcses to suggest exempel good hiking places.
4. **Connecting:** Users receive match suggestions and can send an invitation to connect or join an activity.

This solution is meant to be used on mobile devices or web browsers by anyone looking to expand their local social network.
